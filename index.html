Quick help. Nearby.

ItzB connects people who need a task done with nearby people who have the skills to help — within your chosen radius.

How ItzB works

📍 Find nearby

Search by location, radius, date and time. The default radius is 1.5 km.

👈👉 Discover

Browse suitable helpers or tasks with a smooth, original swipe-card interaction.

💬 Complete & rate

After an accepted request, chat, complete the task, and leave a rating.
